I'm a Software Engineer with experience building and maintaining applications across different ecosystems, architectures, and standards. I enjoy being part of technical discussions — whether that's shaping decisions, planning features, or just asking the questions that help a team move in the right direction.

I work primarily with C#, Python, and JavaScript. Most of my career has been in IT services and consulting, with some time spent in product companies too. That mix has given me a healthy appreciation for context — which is probably why I'm a firm believer in "it depends."

That phrase isn't a cop-out. It's a starting point. It pushes you to ask better questions, find the numbers that actually matter for the business, and think carefully before reaching for an external solution. More often than not, the tools and systems you already have can get you further than you'd expect — it's just a matter of understanding their strengths, accepting their trade-offs, and letting past decisions work in your favour rather than against you.
